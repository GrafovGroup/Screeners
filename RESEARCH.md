## 2. Validated category structure

The following are useful buyer categories, not a list of SCREENERS.CA stock or guaranteed supply:

- **Mobile screeners:** a mobility configuration spanning finishing, scalping and some trommel equipment. Explain track/wheel transport, site access and setup instead of treating all mobile units as one mechanism.
- **Trommel screeners:** rotating drums used for material separation, including organics/topsoil and heavy applications depending on machine design. Organics trommels and heavy rock trommels need separate selection guidance.
- **Vibrating screeners:** incline/horizontal and other configurations for size separation; media, moisture and material gradation matter. Do not invent throughput or aperture ranges.
- **Scalping screeners:** heavy feed/pre-screening applications. Distinguish coarse separation from finished product grading.
- **Compact screeners:** smaller-footprint equipment relevant to constrained sites and lower-volume projects; transportability must be checked against actual model details.
- **Stationary screening plants:** supported by ELRUS and Haver sources; useful for long-term integrated process enquiries, with engineering, power and civil-work inputs.
- **Screening conveyors and ancillary systems:** confirmed across multiple suppliers; discuss transfer/stockpiling, hoppers/feeders and integration. Conveyors are material-handling equipment, not a screening mechanism.

Priority material/application groups supported by the review: sand/gravel and aggregates; construction/demolition recovery; topsoil/compost and organics; mining and industrial minerals; mixed recycling streams. Agriculture and manufacturing pages must stay bounded to relevant material-separation applications rather than asserting food-grade, pharmaceutical, sanitary, explosion-protection or other certifications.

## 4. Practical launch strategy

1. Start with an honest direct enquiry and sourcing workflow using the approved brand/contact information. Confirm each request individually; do not display an invented inventory catalogue, rental fleet or stock counter.
2. Qualify requests by material, feed condition/moisture, desired output fractions, required throughput, site access, power, timeline and destination. Request model/serial/part details for spares. Final suitability requires actual manufacturer or qualified technical review.
3. Obtain regional commercial arrangements before committing to delivery, repairs, installation or on-site screening. Define whether rental is bare equipment or operated service and who carries transport, setup, training and downtime responsibility.
4. Issue a project-specific offer only after identifying equipment, actual availability, delivery, support and commercial terms. Never convert competitor specifications or rental examples into SCREENERS.CA specifications or offers.
5. Publish evidence-based product listings, service boundaries, partner attribution and case studies later as documentation and permission become available.

## 5. Positioning and SEO opportunities

**Inference from this review, not a measured competitive ranking:** much of the reviewed market uses brand/model inventory or a general contact form as its main entry. SCREENERS.CA can make the initial enquiry clearer through a bilingual, material-first selection journey and a single detailed project brief across sales/rental/parts/service requests.

- Use clear category and application explanations with natural EN/FR terminology: screening/criblage, screener/crible or tamiseur in suitable Quebec context, trommel, scalping/précriblage, screen media/toiles de criblage.
- Separate buyer intent: buying/sourcing, temporary rental, replacement parts and technical service. Link related applications so buyers can describe the need even without a machine model.
- Answer practical questions about material samples, moisture, feed size, output sizes, throughput targets, power, delivery and operated service. Explain these are selection inputs, not guaranteed performance.
- Make Canada-wide enquiry coverage clear without local-office claims. Launch a national coverage page first. Add province pages only when they contain specific logistics or application guidance; postpone thin city landing pages and address schema.
- Prioritize functional EN/FR navigation, unique titles/descriptions, reciprocal `en-CA`/`fr-CA` alternate links, canonicals, a sitemap and accessible forms. No keyword-volume or ranking forecasts were measured.
- Do not add star ratings, fabricated reviews, prices, stock schema, manufacturer badges, service response promises or unsupported technical claims.

## 7. Method and limits

Primary supplier and manufacturer pages were searched and read through the connected web tool on the retrieval date above. Ten Canadian-market suppliers were included to span western Canada, the Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada and industrial stationary-screen expertise. Coverage statements are supplier assertions. Public forms were inspected without submitting personal data. Some pages needed JavaScript, timed out, or returned limited text; those limitations are noted instead of inferring missing form behavior. This is not a safety, engineering, legal, dealer-authorization or financial due-diligence report.
