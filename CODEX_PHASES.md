Read CODEX_MASTER_PROMPT.md. Research the Canadian market, 8–12 competitors, relevant screening equipment categories and partner strategy. Produce RESEARCH.md with dated URLs, claim verification status, SEO opportunities and recommended initial priorities. Do not claim supplier relationships.

## Prompt 1A — identity

## Prompt 1B — structure
Produce bilingual route inventory, global navigation and content matrix in SITE_MAP.md. All menus uppercase, ABOUT in footer, FR top bar, wide responsive containers. Validate EN/FR parity and meaningful location pages.

## Prompt 2 — build
Build the complete responsive, bilingual site using a WHC-compatible static deployment strategy, unless hosting capabilities verified otherwise. Include original/licensed accurate industrial imagery, clear CTA hierarchy, category/industry pages and accessible quote forms.

## Prompt 3 — optimize and verify

Run tests and production build; record missing integrations in LAUNCH_BLOCKERS.md. Do not change live DNS or claim search engine submission without confirmation.
